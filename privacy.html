Privacy Policy

Personal Agent · Last updated: October 10, 2026

Personal Agent ("the app") is a private, single-user scheduling assistant operated by Sankarasubramaniam Chockalingam ("the operator") for personal and family use. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar (the operator's own account): to read, create, update and cancel the operator's personal and shared family calendar events, and to send reminders.
  • Gmail (the operator's own account, read-only): to find appointment confirmation emails and extract details such as the date, time, provider and location, so they can be added to the calendar. The app does not send, delete or modify email through Gmail.
  • Messages sent to the app's Telegram bot by the operator and by family members the operator has approved, so appointments can be added by chat.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide the scheduling, reminder and notification features described above. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties except as described below.

Third-party processing

To understand free-text messages and appointment emails, short excerpts may be sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic, and Google's Gemini service as a backup) for processing. The app minimizes the text sent and does not send full mailbox contents. Reminders are delivered through Telegram and email services.

Storage and security

Data is stored on a private server controlled by the operator. Access credentials are kept in protected configuration files with restricted permissions and are never published. Only the operator can access the server.

Retention and deletion

Appointment records are kept only as long as needed for reminders and scheduling history, and can be deleted on request. You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
