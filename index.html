Personal Agent

Personal Agent is a private, single-user scheduling assistant built and operated by Sankarasubramaniam Chockalingam for his own personal and family use. It is not a public product and is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Adds appointments to the owner's Google Calendar, including a shared family calendar.
  • Sends reminders by Telegram and email before appointments.
  • Reads appointment confirmation emails in the owner's Gmail so they can be added to the calendar.

Google data

The app requests access to Google Calendar and Gmail solely for the owner's own account, and only to perform the functions above. For details, see the Privacy Policy.
